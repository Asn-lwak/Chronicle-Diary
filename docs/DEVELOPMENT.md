# Development checklist

- Open the app with no internet connection and confirm the page is styled.
- Create, edit, and delete a disposable entry.
- Test calendar, gallery, analytics, settings, and search.
- Test attachments and voice recording on the intended browser/device.
- Test backup export/import.
- Confirm there are no requests to external CSS/font CDNs in the browser Network panel.
- In the editor, select text and test Bold, Italic, Underline, Heading, List, and Quote; save and reopen the entry to confirm formatting remains.
- Test text Undo and Redo after typing and applying formatting.
- Draw several strokes, undo one, redo it, then clear and undo the clear.
- Open the editor and trigger a microphone-permission notice; verify the notice appears above the editor.
